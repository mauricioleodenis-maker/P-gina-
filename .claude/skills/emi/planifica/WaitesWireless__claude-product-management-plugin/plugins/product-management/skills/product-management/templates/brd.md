## 1. Executive summary

Three to five sentences, conclusion first. What we propose, what it costs, what
it returns, and what decision is being asked for. Written so that a reader who
stops here has the correct impression.

**The ask:** [Approve $X and Y engineering weeks to deliver Z by date D.]

## 2. Business problem or opportunity

What is happening in the business, with evidence. Revenue at risk, cost being
incurred, market movement, regulatory change, competitive pressure.

Quantify it. "Support handles 340 of these requests per month at an average of
22 minutes each, roughly 1.5 FTE." Every number cited to a source.

**Cost of doing nothing over the next 12 months:** [figure and how it was derived]
